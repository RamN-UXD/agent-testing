About TWG

One Vision,
Many Horizons

The Team World Group (TWG) is headquartered at the WestBurry Office Tower, Business Bay, Dubai. Founded in 2013 by Nowfal Hameed, TWG grew from a single commodities trade into one of the region's most celebrated holding enterprises.

After a decade building a diversified portfolio — spanning coal and mineral trading, cement manufacturing in Uganda, bulk shipping, logistics, automotive and carbon lifecycle management — all ventures were consolidated under the TWG holding umbrella in 2024 to unlock greater operational synergies. Today, TWG is a billion-dirham enterprise with operations in 20+ countries.

03Group Journey
Group Journey

One Decade.
Infinite Reach.

From a single commodities trade in 2013 to a multi-vertical global conglomerate by 2024.

2013
Commodities
GCV & Gulf Coal Founded
Established Global Commodities Ventures and Gulf Coal Trading to source coal and minerals for the cement, steel and power industries of the Middle East.
2015
Growth
Middle East #1 Supplier
GCV established as the largest supplier of Coal and Copper Slag in the Middle East, building deep relationships across the GCC.
2019
Cement
Metro Cement, Uganda
Spotting real estate and infrastructure growth in Uganda, diversified into cement manufacturing — setting up Metro Cement in Kampala, later Farasi Cement.
2020
Shipping
Team Bulk Carriers
Launched Team Bulk Carriers (Dubai, Sharjah, Singapore) and Team World Carrier — a full-service logistics provider for ocean and air freight.
2021
Automotive
HMK Parts & 3G Motors
Acquired HMK Parts for genuine and aftermarket auto parts. Founded 3G Motors to supply premium saloons, SUVs and EVs of all global makes worldwide.
2022
Engineering
Cemerg Engineering
Founded Cemerg to provide total engineering services — concept to commissioning — for cement and process industries including operation management.
2023
Sustainability
Net Carbon Vision + COP28
Launched Net Carbon Vision as a carbon lifecycle management provider. Signed MOU with Gulf Cement at COP28 Dubai — formal entry into sustainability.
2024
Consolidation
TWG Holding Established
All nine ventures consolidated under the TWG holding umbrella. Nowfal Hameed formally appointed Chairman — unifying a decade of bold moves under one group.

Our Chairman
Nowfal
Hameed
Chairman · TWG Group

Nowfal Hameed is the founder and Chairman of the TWG Group — a visionary entrepreneur who built a billion-dirham conglomerate from a single commodities trade in 2013. His career began in Dubai in 2002, and over two decades he demonstrated an extraordinary ability to identify under-served markets, move decisively and build businesses that create lasting value.

In 2024, after assembling nine operating companies across six industries and five countries, Nowfal created TWG as the holding structure to unite them — and continues to drive the group's global expansion.
